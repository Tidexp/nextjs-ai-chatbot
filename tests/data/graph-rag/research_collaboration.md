# Research Collaboration Brief

The University of Avalon signed a collaboration memorandum with the Institute of Quantum Systems ("Institute B") in 2023.
Project Q-Compute, grant QG-1842, focuses on quantum error correction.

Lead investigators:

- Prof. Alan Turing (University of Avalon) — Cambridge
- Dr. Ada Lovelace (Institute B) — Oxford

Milestones:

- 2023-09: Establish shared lab protocols in Cambridge.
- 2024-02: Deploy prototype at Oxford.
- 2024-06: Joint publication targeted in the Journal of Quantum Systems.

Notes:
The University of Avalon provides cryogenic equipment; Institute B provides qubit control firmware.
