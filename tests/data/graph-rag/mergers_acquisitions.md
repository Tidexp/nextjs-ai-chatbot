# Mergers & Acquisitions Case

John Smith joined Acme Corp in Paris in 2020 as a Senior Analyst.
Mary Johnson joined Beta Labs in Berlin in 2021 as a Research Lead.

In 2022, Acme Corp acquired Beta Labs in an all-stock transaction.
Following the acquisition, Mary Johnson transitioned to Acme Corp's Berlin office.
John Smith led the integration task force coordinating between Paris and Berlin.

Key outcomes:

- Acme Corp absorbed Beta Labs' robotics portfolio.
- Beta Labs retained its brand in Berlin for one year post-merger.
- Joint roadmap announced for 2023.
